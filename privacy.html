Privacy policy · Effective October 13, 2026

Your information, handled with care.

CUEPA builds private hubs that help small-business owners see their day, collect timecards and keep their tools in one place. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • We only collect what the hub needs to do the work you asked for.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We never see your passwords for Google, QuickBooks or other tools. You sign in on their own pages.
  • Nothing is sent or changed without a person's approval.
  • You can disconnect any tool and ask us to delete your data at any time.

Who we are

"CUEPA," "we" and "us" mean CUEPA, the company that runs app.makingcuepa.com and makingcuepa.com. Our clients are businesses. When a business invites you into its hub, that business decides what the hub is used for, and we handle your information on its behalf.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and the workspace you belong to. Passwords are stored only as a one-way scrambled code that cannot be turned back into your password.
  • Timecards: for team members, the hours you enter, the days, what you worked on, any notes, and whether they were approved.
  • Contact details for reminders: the email address or phone number your employer adds so reminders can reach you.
  • Connected calendars: if you connect Google Calendar, we read event titles, times, locations and calendar names to show them in your hub.
  • Connected accounting: if your business connects QuickBooks, we read the employee list needed to match people to timecards, and we add the hours a person approved.
  • Basic technical data: sign-in cookies and standard server logs (such as time of request and error messages) used to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

  • To show your hub: your day, your calendar, timecards and what needs your attention.
  • To send reminders that a person at your business reviewed and approved.
  • To move hours a person approved into QuickBooks for payroll.
  • To keep accounts secure, fix problems and support you when you ask for help.

We do not use your information to train AI models, for advertising, or for anything unrelated to running your hub.

Google and Intuit data

  • Google Calendar access is read-only. CUEPA cannot create, change or delete your events. Events are fetched when you open the hub and are not stored on our servers.
  • CUEPA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • QuickBooks data is used only to match employees and add approved hours. CUEPA cannot run payroll, pay anyone or send invoices.
  • Connection keys for Google and QuickBooks are encrypted before they are stored.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the service:

  • Your business: the owner and the people they choose can see the hub, including the timecards in it.
  • Service providers: Vercel hosts the hub and stores its data. Google and Intuit receive requests only when you connect their services. Email or text providers deliver approved reminders.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or the security of the service.

We never sell or rent personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep information while your business uses CUEPA. When a business stops using CUEPA, or asks us to delete its data, we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records longer.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar or QuickBooks anytime from your hub. You can also remove CUEPA's access in your Google or Intuit account settings.
  • See, correct or delete your information by emailing us. Team members can also ask the business they work for.
  • Stop reminders by asking your employer or replying to us.

Security

The hub is private and invite-only. Data is sent over encrypted connections, connection keys are encrypted at rest, and access is limited to each person's own workspace. No system is perfectly secure, so we will tell affected businesses promptly if a breach affects their data.

Children

The hub is a work tool for businesses and their staff. It is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell client businesses about important changes.
